▸ PIPING SPOTIFY INTO THE ANALYZER

Spotify exposes no visualizer plugin API, so this reads the actual audio leaving your machine via Chrome's screen-capture pipeline. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the samples go straight into an FFT and die there, unless you press ⏺ REC, which saves a 15 s WebM clip (with the source audio) to your own downloads and nowhere else.

A · SPOTIFY DESKTOP APP

  1. Start playback in Spotify.
  2. Hit SYSTEM AUDIO below.
  3. In the picker choose the Entire Screen tab, click your screen, and — this is the part everyone misses — tick Also share system audio at the bottom left.
  4. Share. Sound keeps playing through your speakers as normal.

B · SPOTIFY WEB PLAYER

  1. Play in an open.spotify.com tab.
  2. Hit SYSTEM AUDIO, pick the Chrome Tab tab, select the Spotify tab, tick Also share tab audio.

C · NO CAPTURE AVAILABLE

MIC listens through your microphone — speakers out loud, phone in the room, a turntable on a line-in. Rough but it dances. DEMO runs a synthetic track with no permissions at all.

Bands are log-spaced 25 Hz → 16 kHz on ISO third-octave spacing. A +3.5 dB/octave pink tilt is applied so treble detail is visible instead of hugging the floor — same trick hardware RTAs use. Ballistics are fast-attack / slow-release with 550 ms peak hold and gravity fall. AGC ranges the input automatically so peaks ride just under the top on any master — the GAIN slider then acts as a bias on top of it. After ~8 s of silence the display drops to standby and wakes on signal; while visible, the page holds a screen wake lock so the monitor stays on.
